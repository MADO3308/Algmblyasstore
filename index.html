The roast notes

From Coffee Farm to Your Cup

Follow the journey of coffee from where it grows to the cup you start your day with.

1

The Farm

At peak ripeness, coffee cherries are handpicked across shaded mountain farms, where altitude begins writing the flavor of every future cup.

2

Processing

Washed, natural, or honey-processed — three paths from cherry to bean, each shaping the character and flavor that will define the final cup.

3

The Roast

Heat sparks the transformation. Every minute and every degree shapes the bean’s character, turning it light and vibrant or dark and bold.

4

The Brew

Water meets the freshly ground bean, unlocking what roasting left behind. Pour-over, espresso, or French press — each method reveals a different expression of aroma, acidity, sweetness, and body.

5

The Cup

Everything before this moment leads to one thing: the first sip — a small pause where the entire journey finally comes together.

6

Beyond the Cup

Every cup leaves a footprint. Fair pay and sustainable farming protect the people and land behind the bean, ensuring the journey begins again with the next harvest.
